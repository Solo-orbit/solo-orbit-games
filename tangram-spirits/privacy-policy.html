Privacy Policy — Tangram Spirits

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Developer: Solo Orbit Games · Contact: soloorbitgames@gmail.com

1. Overview

Tangram Spirits is an offline tangram puzzle game. No account is required, and no personal information is required to play. This policy explains what little data exists and the choices you have.

2. Data stored on your device (local only)

The game saves your progress — current puzzle number, spirits freed, and sparks — using your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data permanently removes it.

3. Purchases and advertising

This version of Tangram Spirits contains no in-app purchases and no third-party advertising SDK. It does not collect analytics, device identifiers, or location data.

4. Children

The game contains no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, and no external links that collect data. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the higher age required by local law).

5. Permissions

Tangram Spirits requests no dangerous Android permissions and does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, or files.

6. Data deletion

To delete all game data, uninstall the app or clear its app data in Android settings. Because we hold no server-side data about you, no further deletion request is necessary. You may contact us at the address above for confirmation.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change goes live on Google Play.
