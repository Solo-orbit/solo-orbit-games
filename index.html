Our Games

NumBlast Escape (数字大逃脱)

A one-touch arcade roguelike. Bounce, blast through rings, upgrade between runs, and escape the grid. Coming soon to Google Play.

Pine Forest Tycoon 🌲

A cozy idle game. Tap to grow a giant pine tree, collect pinecones, invite forest spirits, and replant for golden boosts. Coming soon to Google Play.

Glow Sprite Garden 🌼 (微光精灵花园)

A gentle idle garden. Plant glowing flower spirits, grow them with quick math, and drift through new sprite worlds. Coming soon to Google Play.

Number Fishing 🎣 (数字钓鱼)

A math arcade. Hook the fish carrying the right answer, upgrade your gear and boat, and unlock deeper zones. Coming soon to Google Play.
