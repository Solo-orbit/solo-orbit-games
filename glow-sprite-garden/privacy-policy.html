Privacy Policy — Glow Sprite Garden (微光精灵花园)

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Developer: Solo Orbit Games · Contact: soloorbitgames@gmail.com

1. Overview

Glow Sprite Garden is an offline idle garden game. We designed it so that no account is required and no personal information is required to play. This policy explains what little data exists, who processes it, and the choices you have.

2. Data stored on your device (local only)

The game saves your progress — petals, world progression, purchased upgrades, settings, and statistics — using your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us. Deleting the app, clearing app data, or using the in-game Reset option permanently removes it.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-app purchases (ad removal, petal multipliers) are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. Google's own privacy policy governs transaction data: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Purchase records (e.g. that you own "Remove Ads") are managed by Google Play and restored via your Google account on the same account's devices.

4. Advertising

The current version of Glow Sprite Garden does not contain any third-party advertising SDK. If a future update introduces ads (e.g. via Google AdMob), that SDK may collect device identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID to serve and measure ads, governed by Google's privacy policy. Any such change will be reflected in an updated version of this policy before release.

5. Children

The game contains no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, and no external links that collect data. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the higher age required by local law).

6. Permissions

Glow Sprite Garden requests no dangerous Android permissions. It may use the network permission solely for Google Play Billing and (if enabled in a future version) advertising, as described above.

7. Data deletion

To delete all game data: use in-game Settings → Reset, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no server-side data about you, no further deletion request is necessary. You may also contact us at the address above for confirmation.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change goes live on Google Play.
