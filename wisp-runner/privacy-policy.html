Privacy Policy — Wisp Runner

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Developer: Solo Orbit Games · Contact: soloorbitgames@gmail.com

1. Overview

Wisp Runner is an offline one-button rhythm platformer. We designed it so that no account is required and no personal information is required to play. This policy explains what little data exists and the choices you have.

2. Data stored on your device (local only)

The game saves your progress — unlocked levels, shard balance, owned and equipped wisp skins, and settings — using your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data permanently removes it.

3. Custom levels

Wisp Runner includes a level editor. Any levels you build are saved only in your device's local storage. They are not uploaded, shared, published, or visible to other players, and we never receive them. Deleting app data permanently deletes your custom levels, so keep that in mind before clearing storage.

4. Purchases and advertising

This version of Wisp Runner contains no in-app purchases and no third-party advertising SDK. The in-game shop is bought entirely with shards earned by playing; no real money changes hands. The game does not collect analytics, device identifiers, or location data.

5. Children

The game contains no chat, no online user-generated content, no social features, and no external links that collect data. Because custom levels stay on your own device, nothing you create is ever shown to strangers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the higher age required by local law).

6. Permissions

Wisp Runner requests no dangerous Android permissions. It does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, location, or your files. Sound effects are generated inside the app rather than downloaded.

7. Data deletion

To delete all game data, uninstall the app or clear its app data in Android settings. Because we hold no server-side data about you, no further deletion request is necessary. You may contact us at the address above for confirmation.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change goes live on Google Play.
