Privacy Policy — Lumber Saw

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Developer: Solo Orbit Games · Contact: soloorbitgames@gmail.com

1. Overview

Lumber Saw is a single-player idle sawmill game. It is designed so that no account is required and no personal information is required to play. This policy explains what little data exists, who processes it, and the choices you have.

2. Data stored on your device (local only)

The game saves your progress — coins, saws, upgrades, and settings — using your device or browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us. Clearing site data or app data, or using the in-game reset option, permanently removes it.

3. No accounts, no tracking

Lumber Saw does not create user accounts, does not ask for an email address, and does not include analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.

4. Hosting

When you play the web version, the game files are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record standard technical request logs (such as IP address and browser type) as part of normal web hosting, governed by GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement. We do not have access to, or make use of, those logs to identify individual players.

5. Purchases

The current version of Lumber Saw contains no real-money in-app purchases. All in-game currency is earned by playing. If a future update introduces purchases, they would be processed entirely by Google Play Billing, and we would never see or store your payment details. Any such change will be reflected in an updated version of this policy before release.

6. Advertising

The current version of Lumber Saw does not contain any third-party advertising SDK. If a future update introduces ads (e.g. via Google AdMob), that SDK may collect device identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID to serve and measure ads, governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Any such change will be reflected in an updated version of this policy before release.

7. Children

The game contains no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, and no external links that collect data. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the higher age required by local law).

8. Permissions

Lumber Saw requests no dangerous permissions. A packaged Android version would use the network permission solely for content delivery and (if enabled in a future version) advertising, as described above.

9. Data deletion

To delete all game data: use the in-game reset option, clear your browser's site data, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no server-side data about you, no further deletion request is necessary. You may also contact us at the address above for confirmation.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change goes live.
