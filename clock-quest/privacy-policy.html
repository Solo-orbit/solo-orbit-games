Privacy Policy — Clock Quest Adventure

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Developer: Solo Orbit Games · Contact: soloorbitgames@gmail.com

1. Overview

Clock Quest Adventure is an offline clock-reading game for children and families. We designed it so that no account is required and no personal information is required to play. This policy explains what little data exists, where it lives, and the choices you have.

2. Data stored on your device (local only)

The game saves your progress — unlocked levels, stars earned, stardust, purchased frames and companions, and your best endless score — using your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app or clearing app data in Android settings permanently removes it.

3. Purchases and advertising

This version of Clock Quest Adventure contains no in-app purchases and no third-party advertising SDK. The in-game shop is bought entirely with stardust earned by playing; no real money changes hands. The game does not collect analytics, device identifiers, or location data.

4. Children's privacy

Clock Quest Adventure is designed to be suitable for young children. It contains no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, no accounts, and no external links that collect data. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the higher age required by local law), because the game does not collect personal information at all.

5. Permissions

Clock Quest Adventure requests no dangerous Android permissions. It does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, location, or your files. Sound effects are generated inside the app rather than downloaded, and optional vibration feedback uses only your device's built-in vibration motor.

6. Data deletion

To delete all game data, uninstall the app or clear its app data in Android settings. Because we hold no server-side data about you, no further deletion request is necessary. You may still contact us at the address above and we will confirm.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change goes live on Google Play.
