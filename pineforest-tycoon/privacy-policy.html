Privacy Policy — Pine Forest Tycoon

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · Developer: Solo Orbit Games · Contact: soloorbitgames@gmail.com

1. Overview

Pine Forest Tycoon is an offline idle game. We designed it so that no account is required and no personal information is required to play. This policy explains what little data exists, who processes it, and the choices you have.

2. Data stored on your device (local only)

The game saves your progress — pinecones, golden pinecones, forest spirits, tree levels, upgrades, skins, and settings — using your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us. Deleting the app, clearing app data, or using the in-game Reset Forest option permanently removes it.

3. Purchases

The current version of Pine Forest Tycoon contains no in-app purchases. Every item in the game is earned by playing. If a future update introduces optional purchases (processed entirely by Google Play Billing), we never see or store your payment details, and this policy will be updated before that change goes live.

4. Advertising

The current version of Pine Forest Tycoon does not contain any third-party advertising SDK. If a future update introduces ads (e.g. via Google AdMob), that SDK may collect device identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID to serve and measure ads, governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Any such change will be reflected in an updated version of this policy before release.

5. Children

The game contains no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, and no external links that collect data. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the higher age required by local law).

6. Permissions

Pine Forest Tycoon requests no dangerous Android permissions — no location, no contacts, no camera, no storage access. The app does not access the network for gameplay; the game runs fully offline.

7. Data deletion

To delete all game data: use the in-game Reset Forest option, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no server-side data about you, no further deletion request is necessary. You may also contact us at the address above for confirmation.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change goes live on Google Play.
